Jungian Psychotherapy

My work is grounded in C. G. Jung's Analytical Psychology, understood as a psychotherapy oriented not only towards relieving suffering, but also towards understanding what it expresses and what place it occupies in each person's history.

Symptoms, relationships, dreams, conflicts and repeated patterns can thus become a path to understanding ourselves and producing change.
